Greetings, Welcome to the hiding side of geocaching, and congratulations on placing your first cache.  My name is **Iowa.Landmark**, and I'm one of the Geocaching.com volunteer cache reviewers for Iowa.  Reviewers role is to review and publish caches, and provide guidance when necessary. Part of our job is to make sure new caches comply with the guidelines before they are published.  The guidelines are listed here: http://www.geocaching.com/about/guidelines.aspx?expand=1 for your convenience.  Please don't submit a cache listing for review until you have read and understand these guidelines. As for cache placements, here are a few friendly suggestions: Cache Saturation The reviewers use a rule of thumb that caches placed within .10 miles (528 feet or 161 meters) of another cache may not be listed on the site. This is an arbitrary distance and is just a guideline. Please note that the purpose of the Saturation Guideline is to prevent too many caches from being placed in one area, and to encourage people to seek out new places for caches. It is not intended to prevent one cache from being found too quickly after another, nor is it concerned with the walking distance between caches. As such, we use true "as the crow flies" distances, not travel or route distance. 1. Label the outside of your cache container - this will help those that stumble upon it identify what it is. 2. Include a cache note as part of the initial contents if possible - cache notes can be found here: http://www.geocaching.com/articles/geocachenote.doc 3. Please include attributes on your cache page as most geocachers find them very helpful. Attributes are explained here: http://support.groundspeak.com/index.php?pg=kb.page&id=121 4. If you don't have a hint - leave the hint field blank. It's annoying for many geocachers to decrypt a hint that ends up saying "No Hints" or "maybe later". When you submit your cache to be reviewed, including 3 bits of information in a reviewer note can make the process much faster. A) Please explain the property your cache is placed on. Any cache that is on private property should have permission from the property owner. http://www.geocaching.com/about/guidelines.aspx#permission B) Please explain what type of container your cache is. C) Please explain exactly how and where your cache is hidden. Generally speaking - the more information you can provide in the initial submission, the quicker the reviewing process goes, and the faster your cache gets published!   It's likely that I will need to know more about how the cache is actually hidden to make sure it meets the guidelines published at:  http://www.geocaching.com/about/guidelines.aspx.  At a minimum please include the following: - a brief description of the container - material, - color, - size, etc. - a brief description of the location - in a park, - along a trail, - at a residence (with permission of course), - at a business (again, with permission), - in the public ROW, etc. - a more detailed description of the container location - how high off the ground, - inside a hole, - attached to what, - which side of the beacon, etc. I've recently learned that in some locations new cache listings must include photos of the surrounding area, the container, and its hiding spot.  Iowa has not adopted a requirement like this yet, but feel free to include photos if you have them, it can really help speed up this process. Please put all of this information into the Reviewer Note that you create each time you submit a listing for review.  Also remember, no reviewer will see your changes until you submit the listing for review, and any conversation we have via Reviewer Notes will be visible only to you and the reviewing team. Feel free to contact reviewers directly any time if you need help or advice. During a review we'll occasionally see something that we might need to be explained or changed.  Our preferred method of communication is through Reviewer Notes. Before your cache is published, these reviewer notes are only visible to you and me. If you're not familiar with this process, see the instructions [here](http://support.groundspeak.com//index.php?pg=kb.page&id=74). When your cache is published, the reviewer notes are automatically hidden, and are thereafter only visible to reviewers as a historic record of the review process.
