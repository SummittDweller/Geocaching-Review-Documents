Shutterspot or picto-cache listings like this are no longer allowed, largely because as-written they don't necessarily involve significant GPS use.   Perhaps a secondary concern is that geocachers, especially those who travel alone, may be encouraged to take their eyes off the road (even more than they do when GPS navigating) as they look around for the key image over a potentially large area. So, when we navigate to a waypoint using a GPS the device can be a distraction, but it's generally beneficial because it allows a driver, if necessary, to pay more attention to the road than if they were fumbling with a map or having to read every street sign and following "directions".  In a shutterspot or picto-cache the GPS isn't really useful for finding the image in the landscape, and drivers end up traveling without a specific destination in mind, and tasked with looking for the image instead of things like pedestrians and other vehicles. I have found ways to allow an exception though... if you hide the coordinates of the image (essentially the "key") somewhere in the listing or the online image itself, and make it clear that the coordinates can be obtained without actually finding the real image, then I can generally publish the listing.   I have allowed a couple of caches like this in the Des Moines area recently and there have been no complaints from HQ or other cachers that I am aware of.  But in both cases the solution/coordinates are in the listing, usually hidden in the image itself. You will need to submit this listing for another review.
