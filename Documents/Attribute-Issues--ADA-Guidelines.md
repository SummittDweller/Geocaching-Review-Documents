ADA guidelines for wheelchair access include:  Cache needs to be a minimum of 9 inches from ground, no higher than 54 inches, solid surface 360 degrees around cache location, and a smooth slope no greater than 1" elevation per horizontal foot. If you have any doubt the cache is wheelchair accessible, take a folding chair and place it as close to the cache as one could roll up, then sit in the chair with both feet flat on the ground, and see if you can find, extract, sign and return the log without moving your body. If not, the location should not have the handicap accessible attribute, and should have a terrain rating greater than 1.0.
