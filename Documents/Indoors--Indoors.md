Sorry, but indoor caches are discouraged because a GPS cannot be used indoors and the guidelines require "significant GPS use" when finding a geocache.  Such caches are generally not allowed unless the cache owner provides clues that can lead the geocacher directly to the container WITHOUT a GPS.  Libraries with call numbers provided are one such example.  With no clues of any kind it will be virtually impossible for a geocacher to find your cache without asking for assistance, and you cannot require a cacher to interact with anyone at the cache location in order to find and log the cache. Please relocate this cache to a location where a GPS will be effective (outdoors), or provide clues to the location in the description, or archive the listing. Thank you. **Iowa.Landmark**
