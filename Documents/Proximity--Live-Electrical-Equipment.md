This looks acceptable but I do have one concern.  When I look at the waypoint in an aerial map image it appears to be on or adjacent to what looks like a piece of live electrical equipment.  There was a time when caches on or very near such equipment were not allowed in Iowa due to safety concerns.  We don't want to encourage anyone, especially children to "get comfortable" with feeling around such objects or sticking their hands or fingers into something that might cause injury. So, if this is near live electrical equipment please include a caution *in the listing* indicating that it's not necessary to touch or even approach that equipment, because the cache is not there. If it is on the electrical equipment I always ask that cache owners label the container so that muggles might not become suspicious, but geocachers will obviously know it is "safe".  I usually include a label that says something like "Property of GC, not GE". You will need to re-submit this listing for another review.  Please describe any changes you make, or choose not to make, in the *Reviewer Note* that your provide when you submit it. Thanks and take care. -Iowa.Landmark
