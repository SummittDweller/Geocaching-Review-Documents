It should be easy to reach and retrieve the cache while sitting in a wheelchair, with feet on the ground, and the terrain should be flat and paved. Here's a more detailed breakdown: Terrain Rating:  Caches with a terrain rating of 1 (the easiest) are supposed to be wheelchair accessible, and should have the attribute. Accessibility: The cache location should be reachable and the cache itself should be within easy reach from a seated position (without lifting your feet). Surface:  The path to the cache should be flat and paved or on a similar surface that is easy to navigate with a wheelchair. Obstacles: There should be no significant obstacles, such as steep inclines, uneven surfaces, or overgrowth, that would make it difficult for a wheelchair user to reach the cache. Attribute: If a cache is wheelchair accessible, the cache owner should mark it with the "Wheelchair Accessible" attribute. Considerations: While a cache may be accessible for one wheelchair user, it's important to note that accessibility can vary depending on the type of wheelchair and the user's abilities. Helpful tips: If you are unsure about the accessibility of a geocache, consider using a folding chair to simulate a wheelchair user's reach and ability to access the cache.
