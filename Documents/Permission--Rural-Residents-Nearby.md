I'm reviewing your new listing and I'm concerned that it appears to be very close (less than 0.1 miles) from an occupied rural residence or farm house.  Caches placed near rural homes without explicit permission frequently cause alarm because rural residents aren't accustomed to having lots of traffic and folks stopping nearby to "snoop around". I need photos and lots of detail to show that the location you have selected is out-of-sight and won't raise alarm with folks living nearby, OR you will need to contact all nearby residents to seek their permission.  I will sometimes allow caches to be placed near rural intersections or similar locations where traffic frequently stops, but explicit permission is always the best approach. Note that you many not have to speak directly to nearby residents, there have been numerous cases where a cache owner simply put a note in the resident's mail box or front door explaining what they have hidden and where.  The note should offer to explain what to expect from a geocache nearby, and contact information (an email address is most common) where the resident can reach you to ask questions or provide permission if they wish. In many cases the resident may become interested and several have become avid geocachers themselves! If you obtain permission please include details of the agreement, and contact information for the person who granted permission, in a Reviewer Note posted to this listing. If you are unable to obtain permission please remove the cache and consider placing it elsewhere, somewhere out-of--sight in a more remote location, or in a location where you can obtain permission. You will need to submit this listing for another review.  Please describe any changes you make, or choose not to make, in the *Reviewer Note* that you provide when you submit it. Thanks and take care. -Iowa.Landmark
