It's not difficult to inform the neighbors.  If you don't want to contact them directly consider writing a note (and make copies) to leave on their door(s).  In the note you should briefly explain what you have hidden, or would like to hide, and where.  Leave your email address in the note and ask that they write to you if they have any questions or objections. I recently received a note from a new cache owner who wrote a note and in response the home owner said they found the geocache, got interested, and now the entire family is enjoying a new "hobby".  They even agreed to help maintain the cache if assistance is needed. It's up to you.
